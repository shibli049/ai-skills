# Seniority calibration

Years of experience don't set the level a CV reads at — scope and the kind of impact do. A 15-year CV full of "implemented feature X" reads as mid-level. Judge what level the *evidence* supports, compare it with the target, and say what's missing.

## What each level's CV shows

| Level | Scope of bullets | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mid | Features, tickets, components | Built X, fixed Y, used Z |
| Senior | Services/systems end-to-end, a team's technical quality | Designed and owned a service; led a migration; set testing standards for the team; mentored juniors; handled incidents; made trade-offs |
| Staff / Principal | Multiple teams or the org's architecture | Set direction adopted by N teams; led cross-team initiative; defined platform/standards; reduced org-wide cost/incidents; wrote RFCs that changed how teams work |
| Architect | System landscape, NFRs, stakeholders | Defined target architecture; chose and justified tech; aligned business and engineering; governance; documented decisions (ADRs, C4) |
| Tech lead / EM-track | Team delivery and people | Led team of N; planning; hiring; growth of people; delivery outcomes |

## "Architect" means different jobs

Check which one the user means before calibrating — the CV for each is different:
- **Internal software/solution architect** (product company, enterprise IT): system design, NFRs, governance, ADRs.
- **Customer-facing Solutions Architect / Sales Engineer** (cloud vendors, US SaaS — very common usage): pre-sales demos, customer workshops, POCs, translating requirements, presenting. Needs client-facing and communication evidence and usually a cloud certification.
- **Staff/Principal engineer**: IC track; evidence of cross-team technical leadership, still hands-on.
If unclear, state the assumption and note what would change for the other reading.

## Common gaps when targeting senior → staff/architect

- No evidence of influence beyond own team.
- Decisions not shown — only implementation. Add "chose X over Y because…" style bullets or a design-decision example.
- No non-functional outcomes (reliability, cost, security, scalability).
- No written artefacts (RFCs, ADRs, architecture docs, talks, blog posts).
- No mentoring/leadership signal.
- Title mismatch: if their title is "Backend Developer" but they did senior/lead work, keep the official title but make the scope obvious in the summary and bullets; optionally "Backend Developer (acting tech lead for payments)" if true.

## How to report

"Currently reads as: **Senior** (strong ownership of services, migration lead). Target: **Architect**. Missing: cross-team influence, documented design decisions, NFR outcomes. To close: rewrite bullets A and B to show decisions and trade-offs; add an 'Architecture & design' line in the summary; consider adding [relevant certification] or a public design write-up."

If the gap is large, be honest that the CV can't fully bridge it and suggest experiences to seek (lead a cross-team initiative, write ADRs at work, publish a design write-up of a side project).
